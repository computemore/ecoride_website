# A few tweaks to the design system

## Home page

- The exploration section cards have been ugmented to support app aware bg colouring, still with the glassy look similar to my style with other apps - but now with a a mroe app aware style
  - Each subcard will refer to a feature in the app or workflow
  - Subcard 1: `login -> home -> search location` (easy steps to finding rides)
  - Subcard 2, select your payment method by adding airtel money, then confirm airtel money `select payment tmethod -> add payment method -> choose to apply promos -> confirm ride by mnaking payment -> choose pinpoint`
  - Subcard 3, security features - we have a "ridehub which has ridehub share and ridehub pass, these provide end to end encrypted pins but also provide a way to share your ride details with trusted contacts for safety, so you can share your ride details with a trusted contact who can track your ride in real time and receive notifications about your ride status, providing an extra layer of security and peace of mind during your journeys." `share ride -> ridehub pass` workflow
- Added a slightly moving background image at low size and quality with webp formatting to the Home page to add subtle visual interest without impacting load times or performance
